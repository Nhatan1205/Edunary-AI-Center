Question: How do I book movie tickets online?
Answer: Booking movie tickets online is a simple process designed to save you time and provide convenience. First, select the movie you want to watch from our movie listings. Then, choose your preferred cinema location, date, and showtime. Next, select the number of tickets and seat(s) you want to reserve. After confirming your selection, proceed to the payment page where you can choose a suitable payment method, such as credit/debit card, online banking, or e-wallet. Once the payment is successfully processed, you will receive a confirmation via email or SMS, which contains your booking details and a QR code or booking ID to present at the cinema.
Question: Why can't I complete my booking? The website keeps crashing.
Answer: If you are experiencing issues completing your booking and the website keeps crashing, it may be caused by high traffic, temporary server issues, or browser incompatibility. We recommend refreshing your browser or trying a different one, clearing your browser cache, and ensuring that your internet connection is stable. If the problem persists, try accessing the website during off-peak hours or contact our support team for assistance. We continuously monitor our systems to minimize downtime and ensure a smooth booking experience for all users.
Question: Can I book tickets without creating an account?
Answer: Yes, our website allows you to book tickets as a guest without creating an account. When you proceed with the booking, you will be prompted to enter your contact information, such as an email address or phone number, to receive your booking confirmation. However, creating an account offers additional benefits such as faster checkout, booking history tracking, personalized offers, and easier access to cancellations or refunds.
Question: How many tickets can I book at once?
Answer: The maximum number of tickets you can book in a single transaction may vary depending on the cinema and showtime. Generally, most showtimes allow you to book up to 10 tickets per transaction. If you need to book more than the allowed limit, you may need to complete multiple transactions or contact the cinema directly. Booking in advance is recommended, especially for popular movies or peak hours, to secure the seats you want.
Question: Why is my payment not going through?
Answer: Payment failures can occur due to several reasons, including insufficient funds, incorrect card details, expired cards, or temporary issues with the payment gateway. We recommend checking your payment information carefully and ensuring your bank or e-wallet account is active. Some banks may also require two-factor authentication for online transactions, so make sure to authorize any verification requests. If your payment continues to fail, try using an alternative payment method or contact our customer support team for assistance.
