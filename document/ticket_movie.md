Question: Can I cancel or change my booking?
Answer: Yes, you can cancel or change your booking, but this depends on the cinema’s cancellation policy and the time remaining before the showtime. Most cinemas allow cancellations or changes up to a certain number of hours before the movie starts. To cancel or modify your booking, log in to your account (or access your booking via the confirmation email/booking ID if you booked as a guest), select the booking you want to cancel or change, and follow the instructions. Keep in mind that some bookings may be non-refundable or may incur a small processing fee.



Question: How do I get a refund for my tickets?
Answer: Refunds are processed according to the cinema’s refund policy. If your booking is eligible for a refund, you can request it through your account or by contacting our support team. Once the request is submitted, the refund will be processed to the original payment method used during booking, which may take 5–10 business days depending on the bank or payment provider. Note that partially used or expired tickets may not be eligible for a refund.

Question: Where can I find my e-tickets after booking?
Answer: After booking your tickets, you will receive your e-tickets via email and/or SMS. If you created an account, you can also access your e-tickets by logging in and navigating to your booking history. Each e-ticket contains the movie name, showtime, seat number, and a QR code or booking ID that must be presented at the cinema for entry. We recommend keeping a digital copy or printing your e-tickets to avoid any inconvenience at the theater.

Question: What should I do if I lose my tickets?
Answer: If you lose your e-tickets, do not worry. You can retrieve them by logging in to your account and accessing your booking history or by using the confirmation email or booking ID. If you booked as a guest and cannot find your email, contact our support team with your booking details (name, phone number, email, and booking ID if available), and they will assist you in retrieving your tickets.

Question: Can I exchange my tickets for a different showtime?
Answer: Exchanges for a different showtime are possible depending on the cinema’s policy and ticket availability. To exchange your tickets, log in to your account (or use your booking confirmation as a guest) and select the booking you wish to change. If the desired showtime has available seats, you can exchange your tickets, though some cinemas may charge a small service fee for the change. Exchanges are generally only allowed up to a few hours before the originally booked showtime.
