Udemy uses the following types of Data Collection Tools for the purposes described:

Essential: These Data Collection Tools enable you to access the website, provide basic functions (like logging in or accessing content), secure the website, protect against fraudulent logins, and detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized use of your account. These tools are necessary for the Services to function properly, so if you disable them, parts of the website may be broken or unavailable.

Functional: These Data Collection Tools remember data about your browser and preferences, provide additional website functionality, customize content to better match your interests, and remember settings that affect the appearance and behavior of the Services (like your preferred language or volume level for video playback).

Performance: These Data Collection Tools help measure and improve the Services by providing performance and usage data, number of visits, traffic source, or where the app was downloaded.
