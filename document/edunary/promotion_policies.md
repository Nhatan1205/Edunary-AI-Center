1. How the Promotions Program Works
Edunary offers eligible instructors the opportunity to participate in the Promotions Program, which you can opt into via the Promotions Agreement page in your instructor settings or by written agreement (including by email) between you and Edunary for certain programs, as detailed below. The specific programs offered may change over time.

Not all discount offers or programs may be available in all geographic territories or for all Edunary content. Edunary has sole discretion to determine which content to offer as part of the Promotions Program and to set or update promotional prices. Edunary may remove any of your content from the Promotions Program at any time and at its sole discretion, with or without notice to you.

The discount amounts referenced below are approximate, as actual discounts may be slightly higher due to rounding and currency conversion. Edunary does not guarantee any minimum level of success in connection with the Promotions Program.

2. Edunary Deals Program
The Edunary Deals Program helps increase your revenue potential by enabling Edunary to offer your content at attractive discounts as part of targeted promotions and to optimize your list price. The Deals Program allows Edunary to offer your content to students at a discounted price or list price no lower than $9.99 USD (or the equivalent amount in local currency), except when sold to Edunary’s resellers or distributors, where the price may be lower. For clarity, Edunary determines the equivalent amount in local currency to $9.99 USD using economic factors like purchasing power, rather than direct currency conversion. You can find the minimum promotional price table for each major Edunary market in our Help Center article on pricing tiers. We reserve the right to update this table and change the base price options over time.

In cases where sales to Edunary’s resellers or distributors result in promotional pricing for your content below the thresholds allowed by this Policy, Edunary will calculate your revenue share based on the Total Amount permitted by this Policy.

Discount offers in the Deals Program may be advertised through the Services, user communications, or third-party platforms, and the duration of discount offers may vary.

You can opt out of the Deals Program at any time. However, your content will remain subject to any active sales, campaigns, or promotions at the time you opt out, until those sales, campaigns, or promotions are completed.

3. Edunary Business Program
Eligible instructors may also participate in our subscription program ("Edunary Business Program"), subject to the additional terms in this section. The Edunary Business Program includes Edunary’s subscription-based collections of content for individual consumers and business customers, including private, public, nonprofit, and government organizations ("Subscription Customers"), featuring a curated collection of Edunary’s top content for personal and professional development skills.

By opting into the Edunary Business Program, you agree to make all your content eligible for inclusion in Edunary’s subscription-based collections. We may choose whether to select your content for inclusion in any collection, including any supplements, variations, or subsets of that collection, and may change the selection from time to time at our sole discretion.

Because the collections are subscription-based, we may choose promotional pricing and offer free trials at our sole discretion. While your content is included in a collection, you are not permitted to unpublish it or make it private.

If you have agreed to provide any Assessment Questions (as defined below), you are also subject to the additional terms in Section 4 (Assessments).

Instructors who previously opted into the "Edunary Subscription Program" are subject to the terms of this section.

3.1 Revenue Share

By participating in the Edunary Business Program, you agree that for each collection, your revenue share will be calculated as follows:

Each month, Edunary will calculate the total monthly subscription fees paid to Edunary on behalf of all current Subscription Customers of that collection, less any applicable Transaction Taxes, foreign exchange fees, and third-party fees, such as reseller, promotion, distribution, or payment processing fees.

Twenty percent (20%) of this amount will be allocated to the instructors participating in that collection ("Instructor Revenue Pool") as further described below. In 2025, the revenue share rate will be 17.5%, and in 2026, the revenue share rate will be 15%.

Each month, Edunary will calculate the total number of minutes of content (including course videos, quizzes, practice tests, and coding exercises) in that collection consumed by all current Subscription Customers through their subscriptions to that collection ("Total Minutes Consumed"). For clarity, Total Minutes Consumed does not include consumption through free trials.

Each month, Edunary will also calculate the number of Total Minutes Consumed attributable to each of your content items included in that collection during that month ("Your Content Minutes").

To calculate your revenue share each month, Edunary will divide the Instructor Revenue Pool by the Total Minutes Consumed, then multiply that per-minute amount by Your Content Minutes.

As Edunary provides additional types of content (such as assessments and labs) through the collections, we may update subsection (3) above to specify the additional content types to be included in the calculations for determining Total Minutes Consumed and Your Content Minutes.

3.2 Exclusivity

Unless otherwise specified for Assessment Questions below, when your content is included in a collection, you agree that you will not begin offering any on-demand content, such as pre-recorded courses, that competes with or harms the sales of that content on any website or platform other than your own website or platform. For the avoidance of doubt, this does not include literary works or live teaching engagements. If you choose to terminate your participation in the Edunary Business Program, you agree that this exclusivity provision will remain in effect until we remove your content from all collections.

3.3 Termination

You may choose to terminate your participation in the Edunary Business Program at any time by emailing instructorsupport@udemy.com. Edunary will remove your content from the collection within 12 months of termination and will continue to pay you your revenue share based on the then-current pricing until your content is removed from the relevant collection.

After your content is removed from the collection for any reason, Subscription Customers will no longer be able to enroll in your content, and you will no longer receive revenue share from that collection. However, any individual end users who previously enrolled in your content will still be able to access that content as long as they remain subscribed to the relevant collection.

4. Assessments
Edunary’s assessments ("Assessments") consist of pairs of questions and answers with accompanying explanations (each pair a "Assessment Question") designed to measure proficiency and learning in certain subjects, skills, areas, or professions. Assessments are offered by Edunary as a type of standalone content, separate from courses. For the avoidance of doubt, practice tests, quizzes, coding exercises, and labs are not considered Assessments or Assessment Questions.

By providing Assessment Questions, pursuant to our mutual written agreement (including by email), you agree to the terms in this section.

We may choose your Assessment Questions for inclusion on Edunary.com or in the Edunary Business Program and may change that selection from time to time at our sole discretion. If your Assessment Questions are included in the Edunary Business Program, then you will be subject to the terms related to that program above.

You agree that your Assessment Questions will comply with all criteria that you and Edunary may agree upon in writing (including by email), such as subject matter, format, area, difficulty level, and quantity.

By providing Assessment Questions, you grant Edunary an exclusive license to use, copy, reproduce, distribute, display, publish, and prepare derivative works of your Assessment Questions. You agree that you will not provide your Assessment Questions to others (including through your own website or platform) or license or permit any third party to do so.

You may choose to terminate the license for your Assessment Questions at any time by emailing instructorsupport@udemy.com. Edunary will remove your Assessment Questions from the Services within 12 months of termination and will continue to pay you any related revenue share based on the then-current pricing until your Assessment Questions are removed from the relevant Services.
